    Jury box

    The same evidence reaches each fly at different intensities. Feeding votes to clear, escape votes to hold responsible, and no response abstains.

      How the simulation votes

      Feeding and escape rates come from cached Flyguys connectome trials. The app interpolates mean rates across the sensory grid, scales each rate by its intact-grid peak, and picks the larger response. Equal responses abstain. Seven votes are required to reach a verdict.

      The randomized juror uses the project's cached degree-preserving shuffle of interneuron targets. The selected juror keeps the same evidence in both runs. Sensory scores and vote rules are demo conventions. The result is a simulation outcome with no legal standing.
