What is the fly actually computing?

The sliders scale simulated sensory stimulation from 0 to 200 Hz. In the model, each neuron integrates incoming spikes and fires when its voltage crosses a threshold. FlyWire connectivity determines where those spikes travel. This animation shows the sensory groups and measured outputs. It is a circuit schematic.

The offline vote interpolates between stored simulation trials. The fresh simulation runs 200 ms of model time from rest. A handwritten decoder maps escape activity to urgency, feeding to approval, retreat to pulling back, and forward walking to a pilot. The shuffled comparison uses the same decoder and cached responses from a degree-preserving rewiring. There is no trained readout.
